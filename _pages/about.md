Hello! I am Zeba Afroz, a Ph.D. Research Scholar at the [FLaMe Research Lab](https://flamelab.iiitd.edu.in/) at [IIIT-Delhi](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/), India, supervised by [Dr. Md. Shad Akhtar](https://faculty.iiitd.ac.in/~shad.akhtar/). My research lies at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**, computational journalism, and social computing.

My work focuses on understanding how language is used to shape narratives — whether through ideological framing in news media, incongruences in eyewitness accounts, or figurative expression in online content. I am particularly interested in building robust AI systems that can reason about **bias, deception, and narrative intent** in real-world text.

I completed my M.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence at [J.C. Bose University of Science and Technology, YMCA](https://jcboseust.ac.in/) and my B.Tech. at [Punjab Technical University](https://ptu.ac.in/).

I am always open to collaborations in NLP and computational social science — feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss research ideas.
